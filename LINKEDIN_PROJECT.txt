Name: Autonomous Mobility Network Expansion Strategy

Description:
Built an end-to-end analytics case study to evaluate which U.S. markets merit further consideration for robotaxi, sidewalk robot and drone delivery expansion. Integrated public datasets into a Snowflake/dbt pipeline (27 RAW tables; 686 passing data tests) and scored 35 metros. Tested ranking robustness with 10,000 weighting simulations per mode and modeled regulatory scenarios. San Francisco led baseline robotaxi readiness (91.8/100), while Boston’s scenario ranking ranged from 20th to 4th, highlighting why regulatory diligence matters. Presented findings, trade-offs and next-step recommendations in a six-page Power BI dashboard. Independent project; not affiliated with Uber.
